# Hey, I’m Niv 👋

I’m a Product Manager who spent 5 years in sales before crossing over to the product side — and honestly, it’s the best thing that ever happened to my PRDs.

Sales taught me something most PMs learn too late: **users don’t always say what they mean, but they always mean what they feel.** I carry that into every product I build.

-----

## What I do

I work on digital banking products — API platforms, corporate onboarding flows, conversational banking, and the kind of infrastructure that makes finance feel less like a chore.

But I don’t just write requirements. I prototype. I build scrappy HTML demos to test ideas before a single sprint is planned. I think the best way to get alignment is to show people something they can click on.

Lately I’ve been teaching myself to build AI agents — because the next wave of product thinking isn’t about features, it’s about workflows that think.

-----

## How I work

- I start with the user, not the solution
